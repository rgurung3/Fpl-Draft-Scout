Draft Scout

Waiver and squad help for your FPL Draft league

Find your team ID

On draft.premierleague.com, open your team's Points page and look at the address bar. The number after /entry/ is your team ID. Paste it above and Draft Scout finds your league for you.

You'll then get a personal link. Bookmark it and you'll land straight on your own team next time.

Waiver targets

Free agents who rate higher than your weakest player in the same position, with the main reasons in rating points. Players with a small injury doubt (75% chance of playing) are included, with a backup in case they're ruled out.

Trade analyzer

Pick a manager, tick the players going each way, and see how the trade changes both teams' best eleven, including which positions get stronger or weaker. Both sides need the same positions, because every Draft squad keeps 2 GKP, 5 DEF, 5 MID and 3 FWD.

League squads

Average rating of each manager's best legal eleven: one keeper, at least three defenders, two midfielders and one forward. Yours is open below; select any team to see its squad.

League race

Each manager's league points after every gameweek, climbing from 0 (3 for a win, 1 for a draw). Your line has a dot for each week. Hover or tap a line to see whose it is.

Points each gameweek

Shaded against that week's league average: green above, red below. W, D or L is the head-to-head result.
