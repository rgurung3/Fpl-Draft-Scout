              Is being active paying off?

                Points each manager's claims and trades have brought, against the players they gave up. Bars to the right (green) are ahead, to the left (red) behind. Hover a row for their best and worst move.

                How it's judged: each claim counts the new player's points against the player dropped for him, from the first gameweek after the claim until he was dropped again (or the latest finished gameweek). Trades count too. These are raw points whether or not the player started, and dropped players are often injured or on the bench, so claims look a little better than they really are. Claims for a gameweek that hasn't finished yet aren't counted.

                Trade tracker

                Every trade this season, scored on the points the players on each side have scored since the deal was agreed. A small swing is "too close to call", and a verdict (worth it or regretted) is only handed out once four gameweeks have finished. Points are what the players scored, whether or not they started.
